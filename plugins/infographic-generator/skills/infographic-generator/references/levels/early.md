# early

Ages 6-10 (pt-BR: Ensino Fundamental, anos iniciais; en: elementary school).

## Content Limits

- 3-4 sections at most; one idea per section, one big picture per idea
- Labels of 1-5 words; at most one short sentence (10 words or fewer) per section
- Small, concrete numbers only; no formulas
- Show instead of tell: every section leads with an illustration

## Language

- Everyday words, present tense, active voice
- A technical term appears only if the topic is the term itself, explained in a speech bubble

## Visual Treatment

- Very large rounded lettering; the headline at least twice the size of the labels
- Friendly characters or mascots welcome; bright warm colors; thick outlines
- Generous whitespace; never more than three colors competing in one section

## Style Fit

`craft-handmade`, `kawaii`, `claymation`, `storybook-watercolor`, `lego-brick`, `hand-drawn-edu`
